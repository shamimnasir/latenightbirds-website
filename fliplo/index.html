Questions

Is Fliplo free?

Yes. You can draw up to 48 frames and 3 layers per frame and export GIFs and PNGs for free. Free GIFs carry a small Made with Fliplo mark.

What does Fliplo Pro cost?

Pro is a single one-time purchase, with no subscription. It unlocks unlimited frames and layers, removes the watermark, and adds MP4 export, audio, the speed graph, frame filters and crossfade inbetweens.

Does it work with Apple Pencil?

Yes. Fliplo runs on iPhone and iPad and uses pressure-sensitive Apple Pencil strokes. You can also draw with your finger.

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes. Fliplo is rated 4+, has large tap targets, a friendly Oops undo button, no ads, no accounts and collects no personal data.

Where are my drawings stored?

On your device only. They leave it when you export or share a file yourself.
